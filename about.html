The problem

Adtech privacy law is a patchwork of overlapping jurisdictions, fast-moving standards, and simultaneous enforcement — and it never slows down

Lawyers advising adtech clients today are expected to track California, the EU, the UK, and a dozen US states simultaneously — while standards bodies like the IAB and W3C rewrite the technical foundations every six months.

🌐
Six jurisdictions, one client
A single DSP client faces CPRA, GDPR, UK GDPR, and emerging state laws simultaneously — each with different rules on consent, legitimate interest, and data broker registration.
⚡
Standards change mid-case
IAB Europe's TCF v3.0 is removing legitimate interest as a legal basis for behavioral advertising. W3C's GPC spec just became a Candidate Recommendation. Your advice has to keep up.
🔍
Enforcement is escalating
CPPA, ICO, and EDPB are all actively auditing adtech simultaneously. Missing one enforcement wave means your clients get letters instead of preparation time.

How it works

Simple to start, useful every day

1
Open the Regulation Radar
▾
Developments are shown in priority order — EFFECTIVE rules first, then ENFORCEMENT actions, then open CONSULTATIONs. Filter by source (CNIL, FTC, EDPB…) or jurisdiction from the left rail.
2
Click any card for the full picture
▾
Each card shows the summary, required client action, compliance deadline, and direct links to the primary source. Citations include the exact statutory reference (e.g., Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.185(a)(16)) so you can go straight to the authoritative text.
3
Search across all content
▾
Type any term into the right-hand panel — legitimate interest, TCF, GPC, DSP California — and see matching items across regulations, standards, deadlines, and live feed with highlighted matches.
4
Check Standards Tracker before advising on tech
▾
When a client asks about cookie deprecation, consent string migration, or GPC implementation — the Standards Tracker shows the current version, next version, and what's changing in IAB TCF, GPP, Privacy Sandbox, Apple ATT, and OpenRTB.
5
Stay current with live alerts
▾
Enable browser notifications to be alerted the moment a new item drops from any of your 32 watched sources. The site auto-syncs every 10 minutes from 7 live RSS feeds.
ⓘ
