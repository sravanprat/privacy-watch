The problem

Adtech privacy law is a patchwork of overlapping jurisdictions, fast-moving standards, and simultaneous enforcement — and it never slows down

Lawyers advising adtech clients today are expected to track California, the EU, the UK, and a dozen US states simultaneously — while standards bodies like the IAB and W3C rewrite the technical foundations every six months.

🌐
Six jurisdictions, one client
A single DSP client faces CPRA, GDPR, UK GDPR, and emerging state laws simultaneously — each with different rules on consent, legitimate interest, and data broker registration.
⚡
Standards change mid-case
IAB Europe's TCF v3.0 is removing legitimate interest as a legal basis for behavioral advertising. W3C's GPC spec just became a Candidate Recommendation. Your advice has to keep up.
🔍
Enforcement is escalating
CPPA, ICO, and EDPB are all actively auditing adtech simultaneously. Missing one enforcement wave means your clients get letters instead of preparation time.

How it works

Simple to start, useful every day

1
Open the Regulation Radar
All active adtech regulatory developments are shown in priority order — EFFECTIVE rules first, then ENFORCEMENT actions, then open CONSULTATIONs. Filter by source or jurisdiction from the left rail.
2
Click any item to read the full development
Each card shows the summary, the action your clients need to take, the compliance deadline, and direct links to the primary source documents — regulation text, enforcement notice, or spec.
Citations include the exact statutory reference (e.g., Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.185(a)(16)) so you can go straight to the authoritative text.
3
Search across everything
Type any term into the right-hand search panel — legitimate interest, GPC propagation, TCF, DSP California — and see matching items from regulations, standards, and deadlines with highlighted matches.
4
Check the Standards Tracker before advising on tech
When a client asks about cookie deprecation, consent string migration, or GPC implementation — the Standards Tracker shows the current version, next version, and what's changing in each major framework.
5
Stay current with live alerts
Enable browser notifications and PrivacyWatch will alert you the moment a new item appears from your watched sources. The site auto-syncs every 10 minutes from FTC, IAPP, and IAB feeds.
ⓘ
AI-generated content — verify before advising
PrivacyWatch is a research and monitoring tool in beta. Regulatory summaries, action items, and deadlines are AI-generated and may be incomplete, outdated, or imprecise. Always verify against primary sources before relying on this content for legal advice. Every item includes citation links to the authoritative source — use them. This tool is not a substitute for independent legal research or professional judgment.
